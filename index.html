📋Official requirements checklist
  • ✓Format: JPEG, exactly 600×600 pixels (square), under 240 KB, in color.
  • ✓Head position: head height between 50% and 69% of the image height.
  • ✓Eye level: eyes between 56% and 69% from the bottom of the image.
  • ✓Face: front view, neutral expression, eyes open, mouth closed.
  • ✓Background: plain white or off-white, no shadows or patterns.
  • ✓Recent: taken within the last 6 months, in even lighting.
🚫What to avoid
  • ✗Glasses, sunglasses, hats or head coverings (religious ones need a signed statement).
  • ✗Filters, beauty apps, retouching or edited faces.
  • ✗Uniforms, camouflage clothing, or a photo scanned from paper.
  • ✗Tilted head, shadows across the face, or looking away from the camera.

Rules can change. Before submitting, always check the latest instructions on the Official DV Lottery Portal.
